ABOUT

Building a simpler way
to buy Nordic programmatic.

SOLYNTO is building technology that makes programmatic buying simpler, more transparent and more focused on quality supply. We are starting with the Nordic market and building for agencies that want a better way to access programmatic inventory.

SOLYNTO is founded by Boris Tang, a Stockholm-based architect with 15+ years in large-scale distributed systems, specialized in programmatic infrastructure — including real-time bidding platforms at 200K+ QPS and technical leadership roles across SSP and DSP platforms in the Azerion group.

Working with SOLYNTO means working directly with the person who builds and runs the platform — no account layers in between.
